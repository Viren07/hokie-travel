Clickable prototype · Honors Studio Team 1 — “Getting There”

How might we

How might we reduce transportation barriers for Virginia Tech students caused by unreliable bus access and limited parking availability?

Purpose

A one-stop alternative to finding transportation for VT students. Today, Blacksburg Transit, RIDE Solutions, Parking Services and bike permits are separate, disconnected tools. That fragmentation is the root of the problem.
